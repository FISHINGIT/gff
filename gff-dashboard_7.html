Key highlights

  • This round delivered 85 new Get Fishing projects with £135,547 invested, at an average grant of £1,595.
  • Investment reached all 8 Angling Trust regions and all 14 Environment Agency areas of England.
  • 27 of 85 projects went beyond traditional angling clubs, widening participation through schools, youth and scout groups, charities and community interest companies.
  • Notable partners include the NHS, RAF Wyton People and Families Support, Norfolk Fire and Rescue Service, schools, colleges and local councils.
  • The region with the largest investment was the East of England, with £34,076 across 21 projects (25% of the total).

Key insights

  • The overall approval rate is 59.0% (85 of 144 applications). Excluding the 29 applications with no region recorded, regional approval rates range from 65% to 100%.
  • 29 of the 59 rejected applications (49%) have no region recorded, which suggests incomplete applications are a major driver of rejection.
  • Average request size is similar for approved (£1,595) and rejected (£1,518) applications, so rejections reflect application quality rather than the amount requested.
  • Unmet demand stands at £88,071, the funding requested by rejected applicants; the largest unmet demand with a known region is in the East of England.
The Get Fishing Fund is funded by the Environment Agency from fishing licence income and managed by the Angling Trust. Source: GFF Projects and Small Grants v1 (29 July 2026).
